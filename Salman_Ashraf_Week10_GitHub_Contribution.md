## My contribution

I developed the ethical safeguards and data-management plan for the proposed low-risk online survey that extends my established MIT653 research on AI/ML in cybersecurity threat detection.

### Voluntary and informed participation
Participants will receive a plain-language information statement before the survey starts. It will explain the study purpose, eligibility, what participation involves, expected completion time, foreseeable risks and benefits, privacy arrangements, data use and retention, and contact details. Participants must confirm that they are at least 18 years old, have read the information, and voluntarily consent before proceeding. Participation is unpaid and participants can skip non-essential questions or exit before submission without penalty. Because the survey is anonymous, an individual response cannot reliably be located and withdrawn after submission.

### Minimising harm and discomfort
The project is expected to be low risk. The main risks are minor discomfort when expressing views about AI/ML reliability, possible concern that comments could reflect on an employer or institution, and privacy risk from combinations of demographic information. The survey will not request employer names, credentials, vulnerabilities, exploit details, confidential incident information or other security-sensitive operational data. Broad demographic categories and "Prefer not to answer" options will be used where appropriate. Any accidental identifying information in open-ended responses will be removed before analysis or quotation.

### Confidentiality and privacy
The survey will be anonymous by design and will not request names, student IDs, email addresses or employer names. Indirect identifiers will be minimised. Raw survey exports will be stored in a password-protected, encrypted research folder and access will be restricted to the principal investigator and project supervisor. Findings will be reported in aggregate, and any short quotations will be checked to ensure they do not identify a respondent or organisation.

### Fair and respectful treatment
The same eligibility criteria, participant information, consent process and core questionnaire will apply to everyone. Recruitment and survey wording will use respectful, culturally neutral language. Participation will not depend on gender, ethnicity, nationality, religion or other unrelated characteristics. Questions will use neutral wording and balanced response options, and the limitations of purposive/convenience sampling and self-selection will be acknowledged.

### Transparency and research integrity
All relevant results will be reported whether or not they support the hypotheses. The final report will state the sampling method, exclusions, analysis procedures, missing-data handling and study limitations. Perceptions will be distinguished from measured technical performance and correlation will not be interpreted as causation. No data will be fabricated, altered, cherry-picked or suppressed. Sources, supervisor guidance and legitimate group contributions will be acknowledged appropriately.

### Data management
Anonymous survey data, cleaned datasets, analysis files and research notes will be kept in encrypted, password-protected storage. A five-year retention period after the final research report is proposed, subject to SIHE requirements. Raw data will not be uploaded to public GitHub. At the end of the approved retention period, electronic files, temporary exports and researcher-controlled backups will be securely deleted; any unexpected paper record would be securely shredded.
